What this service is

Scope

Budget Tracker is a personal budgeting and goals tool. You record what you spend and what you receive, optionally set a monthly budget and per-category limits, and you can also set goals — things you're saving towards or habits you're building — and tick them off as you go. The app then shows you what is left and how you're progressing.

It is a record-keeping aid — it is not financial advice, and it is not a bank, payment service or accounting system.

What we store

Privacy

To run the app we keep the following:

  • Account details: your username, email address, and a securely hashed version of your password. The password itself is never stored and cannot be read back.
  • Your records: the entries you log — amount, description, category, date and whether it was money in or out — plus your settings, such as your monthly budget, per-category limits and chosen categories.
  • Your goals: the goals you create, along with their notes, target dates and whether each one is done or still in progress.

Your email is used for password recovery and nothing else. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising. Deleted entries and recurring entries are hidden immediately and kept for up to 30 days so you can undo, then permanently removed.

We rely on other services to run the app: hosting, a database, email delivery and Google reCAPTCHA. They handle data only to provide that service.
