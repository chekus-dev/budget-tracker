If something is broken What you were doing, what you expected, what actually happened. A screenshot of the whole browser window helps more than a cropped one.

If it is about your account Your username. Never send your password — nothing you ask for will require it, and no legitimate message ever will.

Common questions

My data is missing — where did it go?

Check the month selector on the Home screen. Transactions are grouped by the month they were logged against, and a past month is still there even when today's view is empty. If a specific entry is gone, look at Archive — deletions are recoverable for 30 days before being permanently removed.

I paid for Premium but it is not active

Payments usually activate within a few seconds. If it has been more than five minutes, send us a message with the email you paid with and the reference code from your receipt, and we will apply it manually.

Can I export my transactions?

Yes. Open Export and download a month as a CSV spreadsheet or a print-ready PDF report. Both are yours to keep — nothing about your data is locked in.

How do I delete my account?

Send an email from the address you signed up with and we will delete the account and everything in it within seven days. This cannot be undone, so consider exporting first.

Is there a mobile app?

Not yet. The web app works on a phone — open it in your browser and use "Add to Home Screen" for an app-like icon. A native app is on the roadmap but has no date.
