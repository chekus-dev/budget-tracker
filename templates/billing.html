Common questions

Does Premium charge me every month?

No. Every payment is a single charge that adds {{.Days}} days to your account. Nothing is charged automatically and there is nothing to cancel.

What happens when my Premium time runs out?

Your data stays exactly as it is — every expense, category and budget remains. The Premium badge disappears until you pay again. No features are taken away.

Where does the money go?

Server hosting, the domain, email delivery for password resets, and time spent building the app. Budget Tracker has no investors and runs no ads, so this is what keeps it going.

Is my payment information safe?

Yes. Payments are handled entirely by Paystack, a licensed payment processor. Your card details never touch our servers.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, on request within 14 days of any payment. Contact support with the email you signed up with and we will return the payment in full.
