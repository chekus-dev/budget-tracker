Getting started

A quick tour — everything you need in five minutes.

Budget Tracker helps you see where your money goes and what's left. Log what you spend, set a monthly limit, and the app does the maths.

Your first 5 minutes

4 steps
1
Set a monthly budget

Open Settings, enter how much you want to spend this month, and save. This gives the home screen its big number. Leave it blank if you'd rather not track a limit.

2
Log your first expense

Tap the purple + button at the bottom right of the home page. Add what you spent, an amount, and a category. That's it — one entry, a second.

3
Log what comes in

In the same + sheet, switch the toggle from Expense to Income. Use this for salary, refunds, or money from anyone. The app keeps income and expenses separate.

4
Check your progress

Visit Trends to see the year at a glance, or set Goals for things you want to remember or save for.

What each page does

Home What's left this month, plus every entry you've logged. Trends Month-by-month charts and where your money went this year. Goals Things to do this month or this year — save, pay off, or remember. Archive Search and filter old entries by date, category or amount. Settings Budget, currency, categories, theme, and account details. Export Download any month of entries as CSV or PDF.

Common questions

How is "left to spend" worked out?

It starts with your monthly budget, then subtracts every expense you've logged this month. Income is tracked separately and does not add to the "left to spend" figure — that is intentional, so logging a refund doesn't change your spending pace.

What's the difference between an expense and income?

Expenses are money leaving — food, transport, bills. Income is money coming in — salary, refunds, gifts. The + sheet lets you switch between them with one tap. Only expenses count against your budget.

Can I edit or delete an entry?

Yes. On the home screen, every row has a pencil icon (edit) and a trash icon (delete). Editing opens the same + sheet you already know, pre-filled with that entry.

What happens if I go over budget?

Nothing breaks — the hero card turns red and shows how much you're over. You can keep logging. The goal is awareness, not restriction.

Can I track a spending limit per category?

Yes, on the Settings page. Add a category (like "Food" or "Transport"), give it a monthly limit, and the home screen shows a progress bar for each one.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your entries are tied to your account and visible only to you. Your password is stored as a secure hash — nobody, including us, can read it back. See the terms and conditions for full details.

What if I forget my password?

On the login page, tap Forgot password? and enter the email you signed up with. We'll send a reset link.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Go to Export and pick any month. You can download it as CSV (for spreadsheets) or PDF (for printing or archiving).
